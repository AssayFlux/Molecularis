# Code of Conduct

Molecularis welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and implementation, not people.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to evidence and correction.
- Respect privacy, consent, copyright, licenses, and attribution.
- Make space for different disciplines, languages, accessibility needs, and levels of expertise.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and distinguish evidence from opinion.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, malicious disclosure of private information, and knowingly submitting unsafe or unauthorized material are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project spaces, and project-related public interactions.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainers through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile. Include links and relevant context; avoid reposting private information publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation proportionately. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is not acceptable.
